AD sign-in

Choose who signs in with AD, one by one, in Accounts below: Add Account with Sign-in = AD, or "Use AD" on an existing account. They sign in with their Windows account - on a domain PC with no typing (Windows sign-in), or with DOMAIN\username + their AD password. The AD group is optional: leave it empty and only those accounts get in. With a group, its members may also sign in; their account is made on the first sign-in with the role below, and out of the group = refused at the next sign-in. The accounts with an app password keep working (one Admin stays among them in case AD is down).
